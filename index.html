    Small game, lots of room to make it yours

    1. Christmas, Halloween and Carnival, each an optional file with its own palette and emoji food. Turn the palette off to keep your colors, or register a skin of your own.

    2. Colors, sizes and timing are tokens. Change one in your stylesheet and the game follows your brand, no code edits.

    3. Players save a username after each game. Scores stay in the browser, or point it to your own service for a global Top 10.

    4. It starts closed. Open it from a secret code, a hidden click, a 404 page or a loading screen.

    5. Plays with keyboard and touch, announces score and food to screen readers, and starts paused when motion is reduced.

    6. A Web Component of 15.4 KB with zero dependencies. Plain HTML, Astro, React, Vue or WordPress.

    Questions

    Why this one, and not a ready-made snake component?

    Most snake components you can find are tied to one framework or site builder, bring a graphics library with them, or come with a look of their own that you can only recolor.

    • Any website. A Web Component, so it works without installing a framework.
    • Your design, not ours. It reads your colors, sizes and fonts from CSS variables.
    • Light. 15.4 KB gzipped, zero dependencies. Skins and leaderboard load only if you add them.
    • Built to hide. Starts closed and opens from any trigger, which is what an easter egg needs.
    • Accessible and open. Keyboard, touch and screen readers, under the MIT license.
    Does it need React or another framework?

    No. It works in plain HTML, Astro, React, Vue, WordPress or anything that renders HTML.

    Can I make my own skin?

    Yes. Register a skin with a list of foods, a name for each one and an optional palette.

    Where do the scores go?

    With the leaderboard on, scores stay in the player's browser. For a global Top 10, point it to your own service. Only a username and a score are ever sent.

    Is it accessible?

    It plays with keyboard and touch, announces score and food to screen readers, and starts paused without animations when Reduce Motion is on.

    Can I use it in commercial work?

    Yes, under the MIT license. Keep the license notice that comes in the files.
